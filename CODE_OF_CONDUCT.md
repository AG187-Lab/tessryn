# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss technical claims and evidence directly, welcome different experience levels, and make review feedback specific and actionable.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publication of private information and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation to protect contributors and productive collaboration.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact a maintainer privately through a contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not publish another person's private information when reporting a concern.

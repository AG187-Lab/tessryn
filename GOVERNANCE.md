# Governance

Tessryn is initially maintained by AG187 Lab. The founding maintainer is [Fabian Baier](https://github.com/fabianbaier). GitHub repository permissions and CODEOWNERS identify reviewers; this document does not grant additional repository access.

Use issues for implementation work and Discussions for proposals and open research questions. An architectural decision records context, choice, tradeoffs, status and evidence that would cause reconsideration. Proposals remain open until a maintainer records a decision.

Milestones close after their documented exit criteria have evidence. Completion of boot, capacity pooling, accelerator execution and performance are separate claims. Later platform support does not inherit another platform's qualification.

Contributors take ownership explicitly. Maintainers review correctness, evidence and provenance; agents can assist but do not replace independent evaluation or authorize their own software promotion. Performance results publish both successful and rejected placements under declared conditions.
